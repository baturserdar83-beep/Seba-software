FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I get TV channels with the app?

No. Seba IPTV Player is only a player and contains no channels, movies or playlists. You need your own playlist (M3U link or Xtream login) from a legal provider.

Which formats are supported?

M3U and M3U8 playlists, Xtream Codes logins and XMLTV TV guides. HLS (m3u8) streams work on all devices; Android also plays most other stream formats.

Can I use one license on several devices?

A license is valid for one device. If you have several TVs or phones, you need a license for each.

What happens if I replace or reset my device?

The device will get a new ID. Write to us with your receipt and we will help you.

Can I try the app before paying?

Yes – every feature is free for 7 days after installing. You only pay if you want to keep using it.

Does the 1-year license renew automatically?

No. There is no subscription. When the year is over, you can buy a new code if you want to continue.

Which languages does the app support?

The app is in English, and in Danish on devices set to Danish.
