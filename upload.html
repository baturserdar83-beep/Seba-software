Upload your playlist

Send your M3U link or Xtream Codes login to the app on your TV or phone, no remote typing needed.

Shown in the app under Add playlist → Upload from phone or PC.
How it works
  1. Open Seba IPTV Player and choose Add playlist → Upload from phone or PC.
  2. Enter the device ID shown there (or scan the QR code, which fills it in for you).
  3. Add your login or link and press Send. The playlist appears in the app within a few seconds.

Your details are only kept until the app collects them, at most 3 days, and are then deleted from our server. Seba IPTV Player contains no channels or content; use only playlists from a legal provider.
